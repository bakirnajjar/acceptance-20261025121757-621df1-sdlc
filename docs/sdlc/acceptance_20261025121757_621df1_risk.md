## Risk items

**R7B43B-001 — Sign-in presentation or recovery entry drifts** · **should** · Sign-in and recovery entry · `REQ-001`, `REQ-002`, `REQ-015`, `REQ-016` · **S, I**. The server-rendered sign-in page could omit or misrepresent the pictured narrow dark-bordered white panel, heading, labeled email field and `name@example.com` placeholder, masked labeled password field, blue underlined “Forgot password?” link, wide dark button or muted footer; the link might not reach a reset-request form. **Impact:** visitors cannot reliably start recovery and the visible experience diverges. **Mitigation/check:** inspect the rendered response for those elements, follow the link and submit an account identifier; add pytest coverage for the route and entry, without inventing sign-in validation or recovery-page styling. **Priority rationale:** visible fidelity and entry are required, but the image establishes no broader authentication semantics.

**R7B43B-002 — Reset-request response enumerates accounts** · **must** · Request privacy · `REQ-003`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. Existing and unknown identifiers could receive distinguishable visitor-facing responses. **Impact:** account existence is disclosed. **Mitigation/check:** submit equivalent requests for a synthetic and unknown identifier under the same conditions; compare visitor-facing content and status for equality and no disclosure, separately from sink-side issuance. **Priority rationale:** this directly defeats ACC-STD-01 non-enumeration.

**R7B43B-003 — Per-account request limit can be bypassed** · **must** · Request abuse control · `REQ-004`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. A sixth request for one account inside an hour could issue another link, or an elapsed period could continue to block legitimate recovery. **Impact:** abuse protection or recovery fails. **Mitigation/check:** with controlled time, issue five requests for one synthetic account, attempt a sixth within the hour and confirm no sixth link in the sink; after the applicable period elapses, confirm a newly eligible request is allowed. Do not impose an unsupported global limit. **Priority rationale:** the five-per-account-per-hour ACC-STD-01 control is explicit.

**R7B43B-004 — Eligible link is not issued into the prototype sink** · **must** · Link issuance · `REQ-005`, `REQ-011`, `REQ-012`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. An eligible synthetic-account request might have no inspectable link, or an unknown identifier might create an account-specific link. **Impact:** recovery cannot complete or crosses the prototype's account boundary. **Mitigation/check:** inspect the in-memory sink for an eligible account's link and absence of an account-specific link for an unknown identifier; do not require an external provider. **Priority rationale:** valid account-scoped issuance is necessary for recovery.

**R7B43B-005 — Reset link is reusable** · **must** · Link lifecycle · `REQ-006`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. A consumed link could complete a second password change. **Impact:** replay after a legitimate reset. **Mitigation/check:** complete a reset using an unexpired unused link, resubmit the same link and confirm no second reset succeeds; do not assume a mere visit consumes it. **Priority rationale:** reuse breaks ACC-STD-02 single-use protection.

**R7B43B-006 — Expiry boundary is misinterpreted** · **must** · Link lifecycle · `REQ-007`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. A link could work at or after `issued_at + 30 minutes` or expire earlier. **Impact:** access lasts too long or eligible recovery is blocked. **Mitigation/check:** with controlled time and otherwise valid unused links, verify eligibility strictly before `t+30 minutes` and rejection exactly at that instant and afterward. **Priority rationale:** the exclusive 30-minute ACC-STD-02 boundary is a security and acceptance condition.

**R7B43B-007 — Password replacement does not take effect** · **must** · Completion side effects · `REQ-008`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. A completed reset could leave the old credential usable or fail to accept the submitted new one. **Impact:** the account is not recovered. **Mitigation/check:** for a synthetic account and valid unused link, complete the reset and confirm the new password is accepted and the previous one is not; add no unsupported password policy. **Priority rationale:** this defeats the central recovery outcome.

**R7B43B-008 — Some active sessions survive a reset** · **must** · Completion side effects · `REQ-009`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. Only the current session or one device might be invalidated. **Impact:** other authenticated sessions remain usable. **Mitigation/check:** establish multiple active sessions for a synthetic account and confirm none remains authenticated after its successful reset, without prescribing a session technology. **Priority rationale:** surviving sessions break ACC-STD-02 all-session sign-out.

**R7B43B-009 — Owner notification is omitted or misaddressed** · **must** · Completion side effects · `REQ-010`, `REQ-012`, `REQ-016` · **S, K**. A successful reset might produce no owner notification or address one to another account. **Impact:** the owner loses awareness of the change. **Mitigation/check:** after every successful synthetic-account reset, inspect the in-memory sink for a corresponding owner-addressed successful-reset notification; external messaging is not assumed. **Priority rationale:** omission or misaddressing breaks ACC-STD-03.

**R7B43B-010 — Runtime, routing, rendering, or tests are incompatible** · **should** · Runtime and verification · `REQ-013`, `REQ-014`, `REQ-015`, `REQ-016` · **S, I, K**. Python 3.12 execution, Flask sign-in/recovery routing, server response content or pytest scenario coverage might fail. **Impact:** acceptance is not reproducible in the required stack. **Mitigation/check:** start the app and pytest with declared dependencies on Python 3.12; request sign-in and recovery routes through Flask and inspect server responses. Ensure pytest exercises the pictured entry, non-enumeration, per-account limit, link issuance, replay, expiry, credential replacement, all-session sign-out and owner notification. **Priority rationale:** reproducible stack and tests are required; the individual security failures are separately prioritized.

**R7B43B-011 — Prototype boundaries are mistaken for production readiness** · **should** · Prototype boundaries · `REQ-011`, `REQ-012`, `REQ-017` · **S**. Real accounts/external delivery could replace synthetic accounts/in-memory notifications, or documentation might omit that ephemeral substitutes are unsuitable for production. **Impact:** readiness is overstated and tests depend on real services. **Mitigation/check:** exercise only synthetic accounts and inspect in-memory sink records without external delivery; document both substitutes as production limitations, not deployable equivalents. **Priority rationale:** honest prototype positioning and reproducibility are required, but production architecture is not.

**R7B43B-012 — Deployment slips into prototype scope** · **must** · Scope containment · `REQ-017`, `REQ-018` · **S**. A deployment action or workflow could be introduced despite the prohibition. **Impact:** exposure beyond authorized prototype scope. **Mitigation/check:** inspect changes and workflow files for absence of deployment additions, perform no deployment, and keep production-gap documentation descriptive. **Priority rationale:** this violates an explicit prohibition independent of feature completeness.
